Request material from the collection

Every collection in this exhibition decides who may take something out. This form is where you ask.

Fill it in and the slip below is written in the exhibition’s own accession format. You can print it or download it and keep it.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored. It stays in this browser window and is gone when you close it. That is deliberate. A vault that accepts requests keeps a register of everyone who asked, and that register is itself a form of control. This exhibition refuses to keep one, which is the one liberty a real seed bank could never take.
